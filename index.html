Your kind of gathering place

You don’t have to be “a board-game person.”

Some people here play every week. Some haven’t played anything since Monopoly when they were ten. Both are fine. There’s room for all ages, a quiet coffee break, a catch-up with friends, or a game with someone new.

Think warm wood tables, mismatched chairs that somehow work together, plants in the windows, and shelves full of board games, cards, dice, and chess sets. Cards and game pieces are always part of the scenery. The big community table sits right in the middle of it all.

Make it a regular thing

Good company is on the calendar.

A few recurring reasons to stop by. Beginners belong at every table.

Our biggest regular gathering

Open Table Thursday

Every Thursday · 6:30 to 9:30 PM

Bring your own games or grab something from our shelves. Come with friends or come alone; there’s a place for you at the table.

Chess & Coffee

Every Sunday · 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM

Casual chess and a cup of coffee. You absolutely do not have to be good at chess.

Learn-a-Game Night

First Wednesday of each month · 7:00 to 8:30 PM

A staff member picks a game and teaches it. Especially good if tabletop games sound fun but you don’t know where to start.
